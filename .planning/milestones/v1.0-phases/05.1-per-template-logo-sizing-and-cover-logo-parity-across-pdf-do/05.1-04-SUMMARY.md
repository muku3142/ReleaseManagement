### What this means for the phase's evidence

Against the plan's own `<output>` requirement — *"per-surface observations for step 4 (viewer, PDF, DOCX) so a future reader can tell whether parity was genuinely observed or merely asserted"* — the honest answer for a future reader is:

- **Not recorded:** which logo file was used, and whether it was at least 400px tall. This is the specific gap the plan's threat model calls out as `T-05.1-18` (*"Spoofing — a verification 'pass' that never actually exercised the clamp"*), because a logo smaller than the configured height passes trivially under a broken implementation. This is exactly how Phase 5's own parity check missed the original defect.
- **Not recorded:** step 1, whether a pre-phase document was re-exported without regenerating, and whether the PDF was unchanged while the DOCX shrank. Task 1 proved the *stored data* is correct for the 4 templates and 3 snapshots that predate the phase, but no one has confirmed the *rendered output* for them. Note this step was NOT skippable on the plan's stated grounds — those grounds were a zero pre-migration row count, and the count was non-zero.
- **Not recorded:** steps 2, 3, 5 and 6 individually, and the step 4 per-surface measurements.

### What the phase's visual half does rest on

`GenerateThenExportSeamTest` generates one snapshot with a 600×300 logo at 15mm and asserts the rendered box in **both** export formats — the DOCX via `Inline.getExtent()` (EMU, exact) and the PDF via the image XObject's CTM at its `Do` operator (points). It was empirically shown to fail against the pre-fix adapters (`expected: 540000L but was: 2857500L`), so it is a real dimensional guard, not a tautology. 15mm was chosen precisely because the 30mm default would pass against broken code.

What that test cannot see, and what therefore remains unverified by anyone: whether the logo is in the right *place*, whether the cover title is pushed off the page at large heights, and whether the browser viewer agrees with the two export formats. `D-05-07` declined automated cross-format visual parity for exactly this reason, and `D-05.1-07` added a dimensional assertion only.
