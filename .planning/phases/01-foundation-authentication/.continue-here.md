  check, no user store. D-02 records that this leaves the *write* side unscoped — accepted
  deliberately for an internal tool, with a Jira group check as the documented upgrade path.

  version recorded per row, never returned to the client (D-10..D-13). No cloud KMS in Phase 1.
- **No background jobs** — all external access is user-triggered (D-14).
