Thread a multi-select product filter through the existing Releases-tab read model: the release list, its summary and the
deployment history, with a multi-product release counted once everywhere (PDASH-01, D-16-02, D-16-03).

Purpose: the Releases tab tiles, matrix and history must narrow by product server-side, because medians and per-stage
counts are computed on the server; filtering in the browser would leave the summary wrong.
Output: product-aware `DashboardService`, `DashboardController` params with a cap, EXISTS-based deployments filter,
fixture-backed no-double-count tests.
</objective>
