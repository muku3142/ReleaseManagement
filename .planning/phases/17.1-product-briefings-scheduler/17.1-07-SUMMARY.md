## Decisions
- Missing acceptance criteria is "not checked" when the site has no acceptance-criteria field (same rule as 17.1-05), listed alongside "estimate" in the "not checked" suffix.
- If no issue in nextSprint carries a future sprint entry, all nextSprint items are checked and the scope is named "the next sprint".
- Weekly sprint points read "TBC" when any issue in the group has null storyPoints (ambiguous plan wording; conservative reading).
- "Sprint ends within 2 working days" = `workingDaysElapsed(now, endDate) <= 2` (an already-passed end counts as within).
- Active sprint (name, end date) is the first sprint with state "active" found on the active-sprint issues.
