# Phase 17.2 Plan 02: Product-scoped ticket search Summary

`POST /api/tickets/search` accepts a third mode, `productId`. The service looks up the product (404 if unknown), reads its mapped Jira keys from `ProductRepository.jiraProjectKeys()`, and rejects a product with no keys as 422 `PRODUCT_NO_JIRA_PROJECT` before any Jira call. Otherwise it searches with `project in ("ABC", "CNV")` plus the usual status clause and `ORDER BY key ASC`. Release-scope hiding works the same as in the other modes. Advanced JQL is still unscoped (D-17.2-05).
