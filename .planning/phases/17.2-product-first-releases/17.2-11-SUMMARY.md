# Phase 17.2 Plan 11: BUNDLE Grouping Dimension Summary

Templates can now group by bundle. Each bundle gets its own `{{#groups}}` entry, sorted alphabetically ignoring case. All ungrouped tickets go into one "Other" group, and the manual-item category groups follow unchanged. `ItemView`, `GroupView` and `RenderContextMapper` were not touched.

`groups()` now takes the sorted `ReleaseItem` list and the ticket `ItemView` list, in the same order, plus the manual items. The ticket grouping is split into `ticketGroups` (ISSUE_TYPE and COMPONENT, same logic as before) and `bundleGroups`.
