# Sketch 001: Shipflow logo

## Design Question
Which icon + wordmark concept and which brand palette carry "Shipflow — your product roadmap helper": modern, energetic and friendly, between Linear and Productboard?

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/001-shipflow-logo/index.html

## Variants
- **A: Flow chevrons** — two forward chevrons; crisp momentum, Linear-like
- **B: Release steps** — three rising rounded steps (the environment ladder); "flow" in the brand gradient
- **C: Sail** — a small sail boat; friendliest, with a rounded lowercase wordmark

Each variant comes in four palettes (Violet, Indigo → Sky, Teal → Mint, Coral → Amber) and in light and dark.

## What to Look For
- Legibility of the mark at 16px in the browser tab
- Which mark still says "Shipflow" with no wordmark next to it
- How the palette sits in the dark header and on the login page

## Outcome
Winner: **C: Sail**, in the **Indigo → Sky** palette (#4f46e5 → #38bdf8): a white sail boat on a gradient tile, with a rounded lowercase "shipflow" wordmark and the tagline "your product roadmap helper".
