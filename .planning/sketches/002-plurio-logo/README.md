# Sketch 002: Plurio logo

## Design Question
Plurio replaced Shipflow (taken). The tagline is "The product delivery platform" and the description is "One connected workspace for roadmaps, backlogs, engineering execution, release planning, and delivery visibility." Which mark carries that? The sail from sketch 001 says "ship".

## How to View
open .planning/sketches/002-plurio-logo/index.html

## Variants
- **A: Converging streams.** Three lines (roadmap, backlog, engineering) merge into one delivery line.
- **B: P loop.** A single-stroke open "P" monogram that drops a delivery dot. "io" is in the brand gradient.
- **C: Connected hub.** Four nodes joined to one hub, standing for many teams in one workspace. The wordmark is rounded.
- **D: Sail (current).** The sketch 001 winner with the Plurio wordmark, shown for comparison.

The palette chips and the Dark toggle work as in sketch 001; Indigo → Sky is the current brand palette.

## What to Look For
- Which mark still reads at 16px in the browser tab
- Which one says "Plurio" or "connected delivery" without the wordmark beside it
- The wordmark style: plain bold (A), gradient "io" (B) or rounded (C/D)

## Outcome
Winner: **C: Connected hub** in **Violet** (#6d5bf5 → #a78bfa), chosen by the user on 2026-10-26. App primary moved to violet-600 (light) and violet-400 with violet-950 text (dark).
