# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
Shipflow, "your product roadmap helper". It should feel modern and energetic but friendly and approachable: Linear's crisp, confident dark-friendly polish combined with Notion/Productboard's soft, roomy roadmap feel. The mark is a white glyph on a gradient tile, it works in light and dark, and it stays legible as a 16px favicon.

## Reference Points
- Linear
- Notion / Productboard
